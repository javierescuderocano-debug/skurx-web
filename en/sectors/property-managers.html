WHERE CAPACITY IS LOST

What takes up most of
a property manager's time.

01

Incidents coming in from everywhere

Calls, emails and messages from several residents about the same fault, then coordinating with the contractor and updating everyone.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Each incident is logged once and assigned to the contractor, and residents receive status updates without having to call.

02

Meetings and notices of meeting

Preparing notices, sending them, collecting proxies and writing the minutes takes hours per residents' association.

WHAT WE DO · APPLIED AI

Notices sent and tracked automatically, and a first draft of the minutes from the meeting notes.

03

Fees and returned payments

Spotting unpaid fees, notifying the resident and following up, association by association.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Automatic alerts for returned payments and scheduled follow-up until the account is settled.

04

Communications to residents

Water cuts, building works, special levies. Informing each association through the right channel is a job in itself.

WHAT WE DO · TOOL INTEGRATION

Notices prepared once and sent to the right association, through each resident's preferred channel.
