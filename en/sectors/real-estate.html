WHERE CAPACITY IS LOST

What takes up most of
a real estate agency's time.

01

Inquiries after hours or mid-viewing

Portal inquiries arrive at night, at the weekend or while the agent is showing another property. By the time someone replies, the prospect has already contacted other agencies.

WHAT WE DO · APPLIED AI

The client gets an immediate reply with the property details and options to book a viewing. The agent picks up the conversation with the full context.

02

Contacts scattered everywhere

Idealista, Fotocasa, your website, email, each agent's WhatsApp and the office phone. Nobody has the full picture, and some contacts get lost along the way.

WHAT WE DO · TOOL INTEGRATION

Every contact, wherever it comes from, lands in a single place (your CRM, or one we choose with you), with no copying by hand.

03

Follow-up that depends on memory

Calling back after a viewing, announcing a price drop, getting back to someone who said "I'll think about it". If nobody remembers, the opportunity goes cold.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Every contact has its next step scheduled: automatic tasks and reminders for the agent, and follow-up messages for the client.

04

The viewing schedule

Coordinating times with buyers, owners and agents, then confirming, reminding and rescheduling, eats up a good part of the day.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Viewings are confirmed and reminded to each party automatically, and changes show up in the agent's calendar without calls back and forth.

05

Owners asking how things are going

"How many viewings has my apartment had?" Answering each owner, one by one, is time not spent selling.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Each owner regularly receives a summary of viewings and inquiries for their property, built from what your team already records.

06

Listing intake and property documents

Land registry extract, energy certificate, photos, owner details… Gathering it all and writing the listing takes time, and something is always missing.

WHAT WE DO · APPLIED AI

A document checklist that flags what is missing, and a first draft of the property listing written with AI from the agent's notes.
