WHERE CAPACITY IS LOST

What takes up most of
an insurance broker's time.

01

Renewals and expiry dates

Tracking which policy is due, alerting in time and preparing alternatives, client by client.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Automatic expiry alerts to the client and your team as far in advance as you decide, and follow-up until the renewal is closed.

02

Claims handling

The client calls, worried; you have to collect details, photos and reports, pass them to the insurer and keep them informed at every step.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Guided claim opening, with documents collected only once and updates to the client at every status change.

03

Client documents

ID, driving licenses, deeds, vehicle registration documents… Requesting them, receiving them and filing them in the right place takes time.

WHAT WE DO · APPLIED AI

Automatic requests for what is missing, and filing organized by client and by policy.

04

Data spread across software and insurers

Each client's information lives in the management software, in email and on the insurers' websites.

WHAT WE DO · TOOL INTEGRATION

The data is connected so your team has each client's complete record in one place.
