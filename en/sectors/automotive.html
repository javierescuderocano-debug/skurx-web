WHERE CAPACITY IS LOST

What takes up most of
an automotive business's time.

01

Portal inquiries without a quick reply

Coches.net, Wallapop, Milanuncios, your website… When the salesperson is with another customer, inquiries wait for hours.

WHAT WE DO · APPLIED AI

An immediate reply with the vehicle details, the price and the option to book, and the prospect assigned to the available salesperson.

02

Test drives and appointments that don't show

Test drives, valuations, handovers. If nobody confirms and reminds, the slot is lost.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Automatic confirmation and reminders, with the option to change the appointment without calling.

03

Stock listed by hand on several sites

Uploading, updating and removing each vehicle on each portal takes time and leads to mistakes, such as sold cars that are still advertised.

WHAT WE DO · TOOL INTEGRATION

Your stock is published and updated from a single place to every portal.

04

Undecided buyers with no follow-up

"I'll think about it" is the most common answer. Without follow-up, that buyer ends up buying somewhere else.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Scheduled follow-up: useful messages at the right moment, and an alert to the salesperson when the customer shows interest again.

05

Sale paperwork

Contract, ownership transfer, financing, warranty… Gathering and checking every document slows down the handover.

WHAT WE DO · APPLIED AI

A document checklist for each sale that flags what is missing, and drafts prepared with the sale details.

06

Workshop and after-sales

Service bookings, Vehicle inspection and maintenance reminders, repair quotes. A lot of phone calls and a lot of notebooks.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Maintenance reminders for each customer, online workshop bookings and repair quotes sent and accepted without calls.
