WHERE CAPACITY IS LOST

What takes up most of
an installation company's time.

01

Call-outs coming in through every channel

Calls, WhatsApp messages and emails about faults, and every time you have to ask the same things: address, equipment, symptoms, urgency.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Each call-out is logged with all the information needed and assigned to the right technician.

02

Appointments and routes

Fitting in call-outs, confirming with the customer and warning of delays takes up much of the office's day.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Appointments confirmed and reminded automatically, and the customer is notified when the technician is on the way.

03

Paper work orders

Hours, materials and signatures on paper or on the technician's phone, which someone then has to write up for invoicing.

WHAT WE DO · TOOL INTEGRATION

Work orders from the phone that reach the office ready to invoice, without typing them again.

04

Periodic inspections and maintenance

Mandatory inspections, maintenance contracts, warranties… If nobody remembers them, you lose customers and work.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Each customer is reminded of their inspection in time and the appointment is booked without calls.
