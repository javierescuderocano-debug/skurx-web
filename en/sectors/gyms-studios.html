WHERE CAPACITY IS LOST

What takes up most of
a gym or studio's time.

01

Cancellations nobody sees coming

A member stops coming and, by the time they cancel, it's too late.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

An alert to your team when a member hasn't come in for a while, and a message to get back in touch.

02

Inquiries from new clients

Prices, timetables, trial classes. If they aren't answered quickly, the prospect signs up somewhere else.

WHAT WE DO · APPLIED AI

An immediate reply with the information and the option to book a trial class.

03

Bookings and waiting lists

Full classes, last-minute cancellations and places left empty.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Online booking with an automatic waiting list: if someone cancels, the place is offered to the next person.

04

Fees and returned payments

Tracking payments, chasing returned direct debits and handling rate changes, member by member.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Automatic alerts for pending payments and follow-up until they are resolved.
