What takes up most of

01

Orders through every channel

WhatsApp messages, emails, calls and even photos of a notebook. Someone has to interpret them and type them in.

WHAT WE DO · APPLIED AI

Orders are read, interpreted and loaded into your system, ready to review and confirm.

02

Stock and price inquiries

"Do you have any left?", "What's the price?" Every question means checking another program and replying by hand.

WHAT WE DO · TOOL INTEGRATION

An immediate reply with stock and each client's terms, connected to your system.

Delivery notes, invoices and confirmations

Preparing the documents and confirming each order to the client is repetitive and leaves room for error.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Confirmations, delivery notes and invoices generated from the order, without typing anything again.

Outstanding payments

Checking due dates and chasing unpaid invoices, client by client.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Automatic due-date alerts and follow-up on unpaid invoices until they are collected.
