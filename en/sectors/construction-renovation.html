WHERE CAPACITY IS LOST

What takes up most of
a renovation company's time.

01

Incomplete requests

"I want to redo my bathroom, how much would it be?" With no measurements, photos or details, it takes several calls before you can quote.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Every request immediately receives the missing questions (measurements, photos, finishes) and reaches your team already complete.

02

Quotes from scratch

Every quote is put together by hand, line by line, even when half of it looks like previous ones.

WHAT WE DO · APPLIED AI

A draft quote built from the request and your usual line items, ready for you to review and send.

03

Site coordination

Crews, trades, materials and client visits are organized by phone and WhatsApp, and something always slips through.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

A shared schedule, with automatic alerts to each trade and to the client when something changes.

04

Work reports and job tracking

Photos, hours and progress stay on each worker's phone, and the client keeps asking how their job is going.

WHAT WE DO · TOOL INTEGRATION

Work reports from the phone that reach the office in order, and a regular summary for the client.
