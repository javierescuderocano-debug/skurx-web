WHERE CAPACITY IS LOST

What takes up most of
an academy's time.

01

Inquiries in peak season

Timetables, prices, levels, available places. In September and January many arrive at once, and some don't get a reply in time.

WHAT WE DO · APPLIED AI

An immediate reply with each course's information and the option to reserve a place or a trial class.

02

Enrollment by hand

Paper forms, details copied into a spreadsheet, missing documents.

WHAT WE DO · TOOL INTEGRATION

Online enrollment with the details you need, passed straight into your tools.

03

Payments and direct debits

Tracking monthly payments and returned direct debits, and reminding those who haven't paid, student by student.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Automatic alerts for pending payments and follow-up until they are collected.

04

Communication with students and families

Timetable changes, absences, exam notices. All through WhatsApp groups that get out of hand.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Notices sent to the right group or student, with a record of what has been communicated.
