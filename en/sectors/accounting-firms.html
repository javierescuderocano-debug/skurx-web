WHERE CAPACITY IS LOST

What takes up most of
an accounting firm's time.

01

Requesting and chasing documents

Quarterly invoices, payslips, bank statements… Each client sends them when and however they can, and chasing them one by one takes up a large part of every quarter.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

Each client receives a request for what is missing and automatic reminders until it arrives, without anyone having to chase it.

02

Documents arriving through every channel

Email, WhatsApp, phone photos, paper. Then someone has to rename, sort and file them in the right client folder.

WHAT WE DO · APPLIED AI

Documents arrive at a single point, are sorted by client and type, and are filed in the right folder automatically.

03

Deadlines and due dates

Quarterly tax returns, year-end closes, renewals… Keeping track of it all with spreadsheets and memory is a constant risk.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

A calendar of obligations for each client, with alerts to your team and the client as far in advance as you decide.

04

The same questions, over and over

"What do you need from me?", "When is it filed?", "Did you get what I sent?" Every answer interrupts the work.

WHAT WE DO · APPLIED AI

Common questions are answered immediately with each client's information, and anything that needs judgment goes to the advisor with the context.

05

Data typed in twice

What arrives on an invoice gets typed into the accounting software, and then again somewhere else.

WHAT WE DO · TOOL INTEGRATION

Data is extracted from the documents and passed to your management software without being typed twice.

06

Onboarding new clients

Engagement letter, direct debit mandates, initial documents, setting up in your software… Each new client is a small manual project.

WHAT WE DO · AUTOMATION

A guided onboarding process: the client knows what to provide, signs what is needed and is set up in your tools.
