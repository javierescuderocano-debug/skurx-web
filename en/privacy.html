LEGAL INFORMATION

Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Javier Escudero Cano, who trades under the name SKURX SYSTEMS. For any question about your data, write to info@skurx.es.

What data we process

Only what you choose to share with us:

  • Through Fini, the site's assistant (currently in Spanish only): your name, the contact method you choose (email or phone), your preferred time and what you tell us about your company and your situation, together with the full conversation.
  • By email: the details you include in your message.

We do not ask for special categories of data. Please do not include them in your messages.

What we use it for

To answer your inquiry, get in touch with you and, if you ask us to, prepare a proposal. We do not use it to send you advertising, and we do not sell it or pass it on to third parties for commercial purposes.

Legal basis

We process your data because you send it to us voluntarily so that we can contact you (your consent) and, where applicable, to take steps at your request prior to a possible contract.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to answer your inquiry. If we don't end up working together, we delete it no later than twelve months after our last contact. If we start a professional relationship, we keep it for as long as it lasts and for the periods required by law.

Who else is involved

To run the website and the assistant we rely on providers that only process data to deliver their service to us:

  • Web3Forms: sends the conversation you confirm in Fini to our inbox.
  • Microsoft 365: hosts the info@skurx.es mailbox.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website and may log technical connection data, such as your IP address, for security reasons.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In that case, the transfer must be covered by the safeguards set out in the General Data Protection Regulation.

What is stored in your browser

Fini stores the conversation in your own browser for up to thirty days, so you can pick it up again if you come back. We do not receive that information until you confirm sending it, and you can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser. The website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format, or withdraw your consent at any time. Just write to info@skurx.es.

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

Changes to this policy

If we change anything significant, we will update this page and show the date of the latest revision.
